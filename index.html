Road Safety Tips

  • Always wear a seatbelt while driving
  • Follow speed limits and traffic rules
  • Avoid driving under the influence of alcohol
  • Don't use mobile phone while driving
  • Ensure proper vehicle maintenance
  • Check mirrors regularly before lane change
  • Take breaks during long drives to avoid fatigue
  • Wear helmets while riding a motorcycle
  • Use indicators while turning or changing lanes
  • Maintain safe distance from the vehicle ahead
