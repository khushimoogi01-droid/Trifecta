Road Safety Tips

  • Always wear a seatbelt while driving
  • Follow speed limits and traffic rules
  • Avoid driving under the influence
  • Don't use mobile phone while driving
  • Ensure proper vehicle maintenance
  • Check mirrors regularly
  • Take breaks during long drives
  • Wear helmets while riding a motorcycle
